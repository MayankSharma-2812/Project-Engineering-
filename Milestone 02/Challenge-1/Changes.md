# Changes.md

## Original Feature: Motivation Mode
The original feature was a "Motivation Mode" widget that displayed random motivational quotes in the sidebar. These quotes were refreshed every 5 seconds from a hardcoded list in the backend.

## Why it was ineffective
1. **Low Value:** Motivational quotes are passive and do not directly assist in task completion or organization.
2. **Distracting:** The 5-second refresh interval was highly distracting and could break the user's flow.
3. **Redundant:** Product reviews indicated that users rarely interacted with the feature and it had no impact on productivity metrics.

## Improvements Implemented
I replaced the "Motivation Mode" with two active productivity features:
1. **Focus Timer (Pomodoro):** A timer that allows users to work in focused sessions (25 minutes) followed by short breaks (5 minutes). This encourages deep focus and prevents burnout.
2. **Progress Tracker:** A visual bar and counter showing the number of tasks completed versus the total number of tasks. This provides immediate feedback and a sense of achievement, which is a more effective form of motivation.

## How the solution improves the application
- **Direct Support for Productivity:** The Focus Timer helps users manage their time effectively using a proven technique (Pomodoro).
- **Reduced Distraction:** Replaced the rapidly changing quotes with a steady timer and a progress bar.
- **Enhanced User Feedback:** The Progress Tracker gives users a clear view of their accomplishments, making the application more rewarding to use.
- **Improved UI/UX:** The new widgets fit seamlessly into the sidebar and provide actionable information instead of passive text.
